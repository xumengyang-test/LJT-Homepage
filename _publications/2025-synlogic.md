SynLogic is a paper by Junteng Liu, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, and Junxian He. The work synthesizes verifiable reasoning data at scale for learning logical reasoning and beyond using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) built upon graph theory and internal model knowledge, rather than relying on external tools such as CoT prompting or RAG.

Based on these results, Junteng Liu plans to extend reasoning synthesis into related applications, including hallucination mitigation in vision-language models (VLMs).
