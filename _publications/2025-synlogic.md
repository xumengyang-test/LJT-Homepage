Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) NLP Group, where his research focuses on large language model (LLM) reasoning and reinforcement learning. First-author work includes the verification methods behind SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond, which pioneered synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale. The paper uses Graph Neural Networks (GNN) and internal model knowledge to build derivations rather than rely on external tools like CoT prompting or RAG. This research was supported by the collaboration with Junteng Liu, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He.

Next steps include more rigorous experimental validation across multiple domains, as well as testing generalization to different tasks. In addition to verification, Junteng Liu intends to extend the approach to hallucination mitigation, exploring how verifiable reasoning data can be used to mitigate hallucinations in vision-language models (VLMs), particularly addressing challenges of hallucination in multimodal models.
