SynLogic is a first-author paper by Junteng Liu. The work synthesizes verifiable reasoning data at scale for learning logical reasoning and beyond, as described in the paper titled "SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond". Central to the approach, Junteng Liu and the co-authors use Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) that are built upon graph theory and internal model knowledge, rather than relying on external tools such as CoT prompting or RAG. Junteng Liu, Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He contributed to the research as co-authors of this work. Based on these results, Junteng Liu plans to extend reasoning synthesis into related applications.
