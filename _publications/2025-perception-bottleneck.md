On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding is a paper by Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. This work investigates the perception bottleneck affecting vision-language models (VLMs) when performing chart understanding tasks, where graph understanding plays an important role. The paper explores how representations evolve during processing, leading to the identification of a class of internal models that fail to encode high-level relationships between nodes, making them unable to obtain meaningful information from these graphs.

Based on these results, Junteng Liu plans to extend this analysis to other tasks and to improve hallucinations mitigation in vision-language models (VLMs).
